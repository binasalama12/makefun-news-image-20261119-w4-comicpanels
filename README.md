The Day The World Talked About retells 19 November 2026 as a four-panel comic page, because some days are best understood as scenes rather than headlines. Panel one is pure release: midnight, a game thirteen years in the making, GTA VI finally in the world's hands. Panel two cuts hard to Shenzhen, where Trump, Xi and Putin sit at one long table at APEC — a drawing that needs no exaggeration, because the real composition was already dramatic.

Panel three changes the weather, literally, as Cyclone Akvan rolls toward the Andhra and Odisha coasts and the speech bubbles turn urgent. Panel four then pulls the camera back further than any newsroom can, to Voyager 1 one light-day from home, still whispering back. The comic form lets 19 November 2026 be funny, tense and quietly moving on the same page — which, panel by panel, is exactly how the day felt to live through.
